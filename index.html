Step 4 — What to expect month by month
Month 1
Setup, launch, and early data. The ads are live but Meta is still figuring out who to show them to. Don’t read too much into early numbers — this month is about buying information.
Month 2
The algorithm starts to find its rhythm. You’ll see real conversion data and we’ll start optimizing based on what’s working. Cost per visit typically starts dropping here.
Month 3
This is where the numbers in this model become most realistic. The pixel has data, the algorithm is tuned, and results compound.
A note on these numbers: These are estimates based on typical Meta ad performance for premium DTC products at similar price points. Actual results will vary — especially in the first 4–6 weeks while the algorithm is learning. The $2,000/month management fee is not included in any revenue figures above.
